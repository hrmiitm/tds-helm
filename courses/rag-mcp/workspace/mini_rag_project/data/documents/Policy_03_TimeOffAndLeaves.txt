# Corporate Policy 03: Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave of Absence

Section 1: Accrual & Allocation
Full-time salaried employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off per completed calendar month of active service, providing an annual total of 21 vacation days. PTO accrual begins on the first day of employment and becomes redeemable following 30 days of tenure.

Section 2: Rollover Limits & Forfeiture
A maximum of ten (10) accrued, unused PTO days may roll over into the subsequent calendar year on December 31st. Any accrued PTO exceeding the 10-day cap without prior human resources authorization is subject to forfeiture.

Section 3: Sick Leave & Bereavement
Employees receive five (5) paid sick leave days per calendar year, separate from vacation accrual. Consecutive sick absences exceeding three (3) business days require documentation from a licensed healthcare provider. Compassionate bereavement leave provides up to five (5) paid days for the loss of immediate family members.
