# Corporate Policy 01: Remote Work & Telecommuting Guidelines

Section 1: Scope & Eligibility
Full-time and part-time staff members who have completed their initial 90-day probationary review are eligible to apply for regular telecommuting arrangements. Remote work is defined as conducting official professional responsibilities from an approved alternate work location, such as a designated home office.

Section 2: Schedule & Allowances
Eligible team members may telecommute up to three (3) business days per calendar week upon written supervisor approval. Alternate work schedules must ensure core business hour coverage between 09:00 and 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Each remote employee receives an annual equipment reimbursement stipend of $500 for ergonomic home workstation equipment.

Section 3: Communication & Core Expectations
Remote employees must remain reachable via official instant messaging channels (Slack/Teams) and email throughout standard working hours. Departmental team meetings, quarterly planning sessions, and performance appraisals require video conferencing participation unless prior exception is granted by human resources.
